## Inspiration and acknowledgements

Tir-na Nog'th owes a deep creative debt to Roger Zelazny and *The Chronicles of Amber*.
Its setting, characters, and concepts riff heavily on his work, including the name
Tir-na Nog'th itself. This game is an independent, non-commercial work of homage. It is
not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Roger Zelazny or his estate.

- **Quaternius** models, released to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero
  (CC0 1.0). Source: quaternius.com and poly.pizza. Used for the modular dungeon floor
  and walls, the book and orb weapons, and the chest. See `LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`.
- **Kenney** stone staircase (kenney.nl, via poly.pizza), released under Creative Commons

CC0 places no attribution obligation on the user. Credit is given here at the creators'
